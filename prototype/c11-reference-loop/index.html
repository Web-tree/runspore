The reference coding loop: what a kill does at each step

The question: when your Claude session (or the whole machine) dies at any point of the reference loop, what do you find when you come back, whether you start a new Claude session or resume the old one? Here that means the run's state, what the menu offers, and what work is done again. Claude implements, a command runs the tests, red tests go back to Claude at most 3 times in a row before you are asked, and you approve. Everything here follows the decisions C02–C16. Rows marked (C11) are this ticket's own proposals. Click freely, or follow a walkthrough on the right.
